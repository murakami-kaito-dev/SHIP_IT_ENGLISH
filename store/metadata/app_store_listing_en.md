## Description (≤4000)

ShipIt English teaches the technical English engineers actually use at international tech companies. Not textbook English — the real phrases you need in code reviews, standups, design discussions, incident response, and 1:1s.

Who it's for
• Engineers working at (or aiming for) global / international teams
• Anyone who freezes up in English meetings or Slack threads
• People who want to build up technical English in short, daily sessions

Features
• 1,500 field-tested phrases across 14 categories
  Code Review · Meetings · Slack · Git & CI/CD · Architecture · Incident Response · Tech Interview · Sprint Planning · 1on1 / Career · Remote / Async · Docs / Writing · Refactoring & Tech Debt · QA & Testing · Security & Compliance
• Proven spaced repetition (SRS)
  An SM-2–based algorithm resurfaces each card right before you'd forget it. Just tap Forgot / Unsure / Got it.
• 5–10 minutes a day
  Set your own daily new-card count. A study calendar and streaks keep you going.
• Hear the pronunciation (TTS)
  Phrases and examples are read aloud by the on-device voice — works offline.
• Two learning modes
  Switch between "Learn technical English" (for Japanese speakers) and "Learn technical Japanese" (for English speakers).
• Fully offline, no data collection
  Everything stays on your device. No account, no internet required, no ads, no tracking.

How it works
1. See the English phrase and try to recall its meaning
2. Flip the card to check the translation, example, and when to use it
3. Rate yourself Forgot / Unsure / Got it — the next review date is scheduled automatically

Turn spare minutes into the technical English you'll actually use. Ship in English with ShipIt English.
